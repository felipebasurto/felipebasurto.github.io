# Selected work

One line per item. Each Details page has the full story.

## Enterprise AI

  • AILY LABS. For a Fortune 500 pharmaceutical company, a Graph RAG service over factory equipment manuals that answers with the exact page and section. I also cut an internal ETL from about 90 minutes to 15 seconds.
  • Multiverse Computing. CompactifAI pre-sales with AWS, NVIDIA, and Dell, including an on-premises GPU deployment for a defense-sector customer. I also built a Claude skills plugin that the sales team reused across accounts.

## Products

  • Encore. An independent iPhone concert diary, live since May 2025, with over 2,700 accounts and 15,000 concerts logged. Grows through short-form video that a Grok Bot agent runs through Fastlane, with no paid ads.
  • HabitDex. A Pokémon-style habit tracker for iPhone, with on-device data, optional CloudKit sync, and a PRO subscription.
  • Audio Silence Remover. A free, open-source Mac app that removes dead air from spoken audio without leaving the Mac.
  • Musatro. A browser roguelike built on Mus, in strict TypeScript, with Vitest tests for the scoring.

## Community and music

  • SpaceXAI Ambassadors. Europe Regional Lead and Madrid Ambassador. I run hackathons, meetups, and all-day cafés in Madrid, Mallorca, and A Coruña.
  • Triple Check. My Spanish pop-rock band from Burgos, with over 1.7 million Spotify streams and no label.
