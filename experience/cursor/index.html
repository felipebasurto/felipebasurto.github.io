# SpaceXAI Ambassadors

SpaceXAI Europe Regional Lead & Madrid Ambassador. I started as Cursor Madrid Ambassador in May 2025, then Europe Regional Lead—meetups, cafés, hackathons with coworkings, startups, and universities. Now the work is connecting builders across Europe: workshops, founder circles, and the same shipping-heavy community, at a larger scale.

After Cursor was acquired by SpaceX, the community continues as SpaceXAI Ambassadors. Going forward that is Grok Bot meetups and similar. LinkedIn.

Visual log below: events I've organized, and community events I've supported across Europe. More photos: LinkedIn.

    Cursor was acquired by SpaceX. The community program was renamed to SpaceXAI Ambassadors.

    Cursor Community Regional Lead (Europe)

    Promoted to Cursor Community Regional Lead for Europe. What started hosting meetups in Madrid—hackathons, workshops, founder circles—now includes connecting builders across the continent.

  • ~Dec 2025
    Cursor Hackathon × HappyRobot × Kibo Ventures

    Challenge: integrate a HappyRobot AI agent into a working product using Cursor.

    Winners

    1. TravelBot — corporate travel end to end (stays, transport, dining).
    2. MedTracker — automated daily calls to elderly relatives for medication check-ins.
    3. Santa's Call — Santa calls your kid about behavior and wish lists.
  • ~Nov 2025
    First Cursor Hackathon (Madrid)

    Partners Enzo Ventures, OneCoWork, productmakers.ai. We opened with a build block; good turnout and a waitlist.

    Theme: gamification — ~2 hours to ship with Cursor.

    Winners

    1. RoomieScore — chores, expenses, and co-living for roommates.
    2. FocoQuest — productivity as missions and progression.
    3. FinPlay — personal finance made visual.
