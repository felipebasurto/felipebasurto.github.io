# SpaceXAI Ambassadors

I joined Cursor's community program as Madrid Ambassador in May 2025. I later served as Cursor Community Regional Lead for Europe, supporting meetups, cafes, hackathons, workshops, and founder groups.

After Cursor joined SpaceX, the program became SpaceXAI Ambassadors. I now organize Grok Bot meetups for the next chapter. SpaceXAI Ambassadors on LinkedIn.

The event log keeps the original Cursor names for meetups and hackathons that happened before the change. It separates events I organized from events I supported.

    3. FinPlay made personal finance visual.
