# Universidad de Burgos

Computer Science Engineering, 2017 to 2022. Programming, algorithms, databases, networks, and software engineering, in my home city.

UBU is the youngest and smallest university in Castilla y León. In the 2025 CYD Ranking, its Computer Engineering degree ranked first in Spain for graduate employment. The 2025 U-Ranking rated it the best university in the region for the sixth year in a row.

## Two years away

  • 2019 to 2020, Wrocław University of Science and Technology, Poland (Erasmus+). Classes, projects, and daily life in English. It made working in English normal for me.
  • 2021 to 2022, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (SICUE). The same degree, taught at a much larger university.

## Mus

The other thing I learned properly at university was mus, the Spanish card game played in pairs with signals and bluffing. I played a lot of it and learned a lot from it, and it is still one of my favorite games.
