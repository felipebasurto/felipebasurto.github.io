# Selected work

One line per item. Each Details page has the full story.

## Enterprise AI

- [AILY LABS](../experience/aily/). For a Fortune 500 pharmaceutical company, a Graph RAG service over factory equipment manuals that answers with the exact page and section. I also cut an internal ETL from about 90 minutes to 15 seconds.
- [Multiverse Computing](../experience/multiverse/). CompactifAI pre-sales with AWS, NVIDIA, and Dell, including an on-premises GPU deployment for a defense-sector customer. I also built a Claude skills plugin that the sales team reused across accounts.

## Products

- [Encore](../experience/encore/). An independent iPhone concert diary, live since May 2025, with over €4,000 in revenue and no ad budget.
- [HabitDex](../experience/habitdex/). A Pokémon-style habit tracker for iPhone, with on-device data, optional CloudKit sync, and a PRO subscription.
- [Audio Silence Remover](../experience/audio-silence-remover/). A free, open-source Mac app that removes dead air from spoken audio without leaving the Mac.
- [Musatro](../experience/musatro/). An unfinished browser roguelike built on Mus, in strict TypeScript, with Vitest tests for the scoring.

## Community and music

- [SpaceXAI Ambassadors](../experience/cursor/). Europe Regional Lead and Madrid Ambassador. I run hackathons, meetups, and all-day cafés in Madrid, Mallorca, and A Coruña.
- [Triple Check](../triplecheck/). My Spanish pop-rock band from Burgos, with over 1.7 million Spotify streams and no label.
