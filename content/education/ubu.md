# Universidad de Burgos

![Universidad de Burgos](../../assets/companies/ubu.png)

**Computer Science Engineering, 2017 to 2022.** Programming, algorithms, databases, networks, and software engineering, in my home city.

UBU is the youngest and smallest university in Castilla y León. In the [2025 CYD Ranking](https://www.fundacioncyd.org/las-universidades-mas-destacadas-para-estudiar-informatica-e-ingenieria-electronica-y-de-telecomunicacion-segun-el-ranking-cyd/), its Computer Engineering degree ranked first in Spain for graduate employment. The [2025 U-Ranking](https://www.ubu.es/noticias/la-ubu-es-por-sexto-ano-la-universidad-mejor-valorada-de-castilla-y-leon) rated it the best university in the region for the sixth year in a row.

## Two years away

- ![Wrocław University of Science and Technology](../../assets/companies/pwr.jpg)**2019 to 2020, Wrocław University of Science and Technology, Poland (Erasmus+).** Classes, projects, and daily life in English. It made working in English normal for me.
- ![Universidad Autónoma de Madrid](../../assets/companies/uam.png)**2021 to 2022, Universidad Autónoma de Madrid (SICUE).** The same degree, taught at a much larger university.

## Mus

The other thing I learned properly at university was mus, the Spanish card game played in pairs with signals and bluffing. I played a lot of it and learned a lot from it, and it is still one of my favorite games.
