# Multiverse Computing

![Multiverse](../../assets/companies/multiverse.png)

**Previous role: Solutions Architect, Nov 2025 to July 2026.** I worked in Madrid on technical pre-sales for CompactifAI, Multiverse Computing's LLM compression product.

A discovery session usually started with three inputs: the model the customer wanted to run, the hardware they had, and how much quality they could give up. I compared memory use, latency, inference cost, and quality loss, then wrote a recommendation an engineer could test and an executive could price.

I built and presented the demos and proofs of concept for customer meetings, webinars, and events. The same result had to work for an ML engineer asking about benchmarks and for a manager asking what it would cost per month.

Deployment planning ran with AWS, NVIDIA, and Dell, for both cloud and on-premises setups. I contributed to an on-premises GPU deployment for a defense-sector customer.

Most deals involved engineering, infrastructure, procurement, and leadership, each with its own concern. A good part of the job was getting them to one decision, then writing it down as a reference architecture the partners could build.

## Reusable sales tooling

I built a Claude skills plugin for the sales and presales team, so they could run the same workflows on every account without rewriting prompts.

- Prepare customer context and talking points before a call.
- Map consortium work packages to Multiverse capabilities in multi-vendor bids.
- Generate PowerPoint, Excel, Word, PDF, and HTML files from shared company templates.

The plugin loaded approved company context, product positioning, visual assets, and team roles. A call brief and a one-page proposal used the same facts and the same wording. A seller got a usable first draft, and a solutions architect could trace each technical claim back to its source.
