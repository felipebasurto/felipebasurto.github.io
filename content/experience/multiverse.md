# Multiverse Computing

![Multiverse](../../assets/companies/multiverse.png)

**Solutions Architect, Nov 2025 to July 2026, Madrid.** I ran technical pre-sales for CompactifAI, Multiverse Computing's LLM compression product: discovery, demos, proofs of concept, and deployment plans.

## The company

Multiverse started in San Sebastián with quantum-inspired algorithms and moved into AI model compression. CompactifAI uses tensor networks to shrink large language models by up to 95% while keeping their performance, so they run on less hardware at a lower cost.

- **June 2025.** [$215M Series B](https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/12/multiverse-computing-raises-215m-for-tech-that-could-radically-slim-ai-costs/) led by Bullhound Capital, with HP Tech Ventures, Toshiba, Forgepoint, and Santander among the investors. I joined a few months later.
- **July 2026.** [Series C targeting up to $570M](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/28/ai-model-compression-startup-multiverse-raises-570m-1-7b-valuation/) at a $1.7B pre-money valuation, about five times the Series B. It was co-led by Forgepoint, BNPP Solar Impulse Venture Fund, and Bullhound, and announced the month my role ended.

## Pre-sales

A discovery session usually started with three inputs: the model the customer wanted to run, the hardware they had, and how much quality they could give up. I compared memory use, latency, inference cost, and quality loss, then wrote a recommendation an engineer could test and an executive could price.

I built and presented the demos for customer meetings, webinars, and events. The same result had to make sense to an ML engineer asking about benchmarks and to a manager asking what it would cost per month.

Most deals involved engineering, infrastructure, procurement, and leadership, each with its own concern. A good part of the job was getting them to one decision and writing it down as a reference architecture.

## Deployments

Deployment planning ran with AWS, NVIDIA, and Dell, for both cloud and on-premises setups. I contributed to an on-premises GPU deployment for a defense-sector customer.

## Sales tooling

I built a Claude skills plugin for the sales and presales team, so they could run the same workflows on every account without rewriting prompts.

- Prepare customer context and talking points before a call.
- Map consortium work packages to Multiverse capabilities in multi-vendor bids.
- Generate PowerPoint, Excel, Word, PDF, and HTML files from shared company templates.

The plugin loaded approved company context, product positioning, visual assets, and team roles, so a call brief and a one-page proposal used the same facts and wording. A seller started from a usable draft, and a solutions architect could trace each technical claim back to its source.
